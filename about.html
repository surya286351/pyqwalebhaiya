About PYQWaleBhaiya

PYQWaleBhaiya is a student-focused educational platform created to make Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) easier to find, organize and access.

The platform is designed especially for students preparing for AKTU B.Tech examinations. Our goal is to provide a simple and organized interface where students can find available question papers without having to search through multiple sources.

Our Purpose

Previous year question papers are useful study resources for understanding exam patterns, identifying frequently asked topics and practicing questions. PYQWaleBhaiya organizes available papers in a folder-style interface to make this process easier for students.

What We Provide

The website provides access to available educational resources including previous year question papers. These resources are shared through Google Drive and linked or embedded on the website for convenient access.

Simple & Student-Friendly

We aim to keep PYQWaleBhaiya lightweight, mobile-friendly and easy to navigate. Students can browse academic years and access the available question papers from a single platform.

Our Goal

Our goal is to continuously improve the organization and accessibility of educational resources while keeping the platform simple and useful for students.
